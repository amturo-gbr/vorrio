Privacy policy

What the lightweight Vorrio project website processes – and what it deliberately does not.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

At a glance: Necessary technologies provide the website and consent management. Google Analytics 4 loads only after voluntary consent. Without consent, this website does not connect to Google Analytics. The selection can be changed or withdrawn at any time through “Privacy settings” in the footer.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the public Vorrio project website. Vorrio itself is a self-hosted application. The operator of each third-party Vorrio installation is solely responsible for its data processing. Product-specific information is available in the product privacy documentation.

3. Provision and hosting

The project website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, United States. When the website is requested, Vercel processes technically necessary connection and log data. This may include the IP address, timestamp, requested resource, referrer, browser and device information. Processing serves the secure, stable and error-free provision of the website and relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Vercel processes data on our behalf under the applicable contractual and data-protection terms. Processing in the United States cannot be excluded. Vercel identifies the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, where required, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as transfer mechanisms. Retention depends on the period required for delivery, security, troubleshooting and legal or contractual obligations. Further information is available in Vercel's Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum.

4. Consent management with Usercentrics

We use the Usercentrics Consent Management Platform provided by Usercentrics GmbH, Sendlinger Straße 7, 80331 Munich, Germany, to obtain, manage and document consent. Data processed may include a consent ID, the time and content of consent or refusal, banner language, configuration and template version, and device, browser, referrer and IP information. Technically necessary browser storage remembers the selection.

Processing fulfils our documentation obligations under Article 7(1) GDPR and relies on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. We additionally rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for the technically necessary provision and security of consent management. Usercentrics acts as our processor. According to Usercentrics, consent data is generally retained for one year and processing primarily takes place in the European Union. Transfers to the United States through subprocessors cannot be fully excluded and are safeguarded by the applicable transfer mechanisms, in particular Standard Contractual Clauses. See the Usercentrics Privacy Policy.

5. Google Analytics 4 and Consent Mode

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, to evaluate page views, scroll depth and clicks on external links. Data processed may include the requested page, referrer, time, browser and device information, session information and an approximate region derived from the IP address. Google states that IP addresses of users in the EU are used for geolocation during collection and discarded before being stored.

The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where information is stored on or read from your device, § 25(1) TDDDG. According to Google, the GA4 cookies _ga and _ga_<container-id> have a default lifetime of up to two years. User-level and event-level retention in our property is limited to two months and is not reset on new activity. Google signals, user-provided data collection, ads personalisation, User-ID and Google Ads are not enabled.

We use Basic Google Consent Mode v2. Before a decision, analytics and advertising consent start as “denied” and the Google tag is not loaded; no cookieless analytics pings are sent. Only consent to “Google Analytics 4” grants analytics_storage. Advertising consent values remain denied. Recipients may include Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC and other affiliated companies or processors. Processing in the United States is possible; Google refers in particular to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

You can change or withdraw consent at any time with future effect through “Privacy settings” in the footer. Without consent or after withdrawal, this website will not send new analytics data to Google Analytics. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Voluntary support through Stripe

Visitors who choose to support Vorrio financially are redirected to a Stripe-hosted payment page only after actively selecting a support button. The project website does not embed a Stripe script and does not transmit payment data to Stripe merely by being visited. On the Stripe page, Stripe processes contact, payment, transaction, device and fraud-prevention data in order to handle the requested one-time or monthly payment. The payment recipient is Amturo UG (haftungsbeschränkt).

Processing required to handle the requested payment relies on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Statutory retention and evidence obligations rely on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, while abuse and fraud prevention rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Payment and accounting records are retained for the applicable commercial and tax-law periods. Stripe may also process data outside the European Economic Area and describes its transfer mechanisms in its Privacy Policy.

7. External links

The website links to external services, in particular GitHub. A connection to the relevant provider is only established after a visitor follows such a link. The external provider is responsible for its processing. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for more information.

8. Contact by email or phone

When you contact us, we process the information you provide to handle and answer your request. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where your request concerns a contract or pre-contractual measures. In other cases, processing relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the appropriate handling of your request.

We delete the information once the request has been resolved, unless legal retention obligations or legitimate reasons require further storage.

9. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise these rights, email info@amturo.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Our competent authority is generally the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany.

10. Security and updates

TLS encryption is used for the website. We update this policy when hosting, services, law or processing activities change.
