Privacy policy

What the lightweight Vorrio project website processes – and what it deliberately does not.

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Current implementation: This website uses no analytics or marketing services, no cookies, and no local or session storage. It therefore does not show a consent banner. The static project website is hosted by Vercel. Its legal and contractual details should receive a final review before the public announcement.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the public Vorrio project website. Vorrio itself is a self-hosted application. The operator of each third-party Vorrio installation is solely responsible for its data processing. Product-specific information is available in the product privacy documentation.

3. Provision and hosting

The project website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, United States. When the website is requested, Vercel processes technically necessary connection and log data. This may include the IP address, timestamp, requested resource, referrer, browser and device information. Processing serves the secure, stable and error-free provision of the website and relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Vercel processes data on our behalf under the applicable contractual and data-protection terms. Processing in the United States cannot be excluded. Vercel identifies the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, where required, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as transfer mechanisms. Retention depends on the period required for delivery, security, troubleshooting and legal or contractual obligations. Further information is available in Vercel's Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum.

4. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The current website does not set cookies or access information on the device through local storage or session storage. It does not create usage profiles or load analytics, advertising or tracking services. Fonts, scripts and images are served as local website files.

No consent under § 25(1) TDDDG is required for this implementation, so no cookie banner is provided. If a technology requiring consent is added later, it will only be loaded after valid consent and this policy will be updated.

5. Financial support through Stripe

Once financial support is activated, the corresponding controls lead to a Stripe-hosted payment page. The Vorrio website itself does not embed Stripe scripts. A connection to Stripe is only established when you follow such a link.

We use Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland, to process payments. Stripe processes data including contact, payment, device, IP and transaction information. We receive the transaction information required for processing, receipts, refunds and accounting, but not complete card details.

Processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for the payment requested by you. Where processing serves fraud prevention, security and legal defence, we rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Stripe may process data outside the EEA and states that it uses appropriate transfer mechanisms. See the Stripe Privacy Policy for more information.

6. External links

The website links to external services, in particular GitHub and, once activated, the Stripe payment page. A connection to the relevant provider is only established after a visitor follows such a link. The external provider is responsible for its processing. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for more information.

7. Contact by email or phone

When you contact us, we process the information you provide to handle and answer your request. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where your request concerns a contract or pre-contractual measures. In other cases, processing relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the appropriate handling of your request.

We delete the information once the request has been resolved, unless legal retention obligations or legitimate reasons require further storage.

8. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise these rights, email info@amturo.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Our competent authority is generally the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany.

9. Security and updates

TLS encryption will be used for the public website. We update this policy when hosting, services, law or processing activities change. Before the public launch, this version must be legally reviewed together with the final hosting setup and concluded agreements.
