# Community code of conduct

Vorrio welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people of every
background and experience level.

Be kind, stay focused on the technical or household workflow, assume good
faith, and give feedback about behavior rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of
private information and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Never post another person's receipt, address, credential, household activity
or other private data. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the Amturo repository maintainers.
Reports are reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.
